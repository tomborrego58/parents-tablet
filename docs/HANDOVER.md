## Part 1: Tom's Final Handover & Maintenance Reference

### 1. Remaining Pre-Delivery Steps
- **Google Meet 1-time calls (on tablet):** Open Meet once and place a quick call to **Tom**, **Katie**, **Laura**, and **Matt** so all 4 stay pinned at the top of Meet's main screen.
- **Verify latest update on tablet:** Swipe from the **left edge** in Fully Kiosk → tap **Go to Start URL**.
- **Print the 1-page guide:** Open [`docs/cheat-sheet.html`](./cheat-sheet.html) → click **🖨️ Print 1-Page Guide** (set Margins to *Default* or *Minimum*, uncheck *Headers and footers*).
- **Optional Settings (`⚙️ Settings` tab):**
  - Add **Doctor Name** & **Doctor Phone Number** (adds a red Doctor phone card on `📞 Call`).
  - Paste a **Video Messages Drive Folder URL** (shared *Anyone with the link → Viewer*) if family wants to drop short video messages for Gene & Peggy.

### 2. How Day-to-Day Maintenance Works (From Your Phone or PC)
- **Adding / organizing family photos:**
  - Drop photos into `Parent Tablet Pics` in Google Drive (or into subfolders like `Boys`, `Matt and Katie`, `Laura and Tom` — subfolder buttons appear automatically on the left side of **🖼️ Pictures**).
  - Or email photo attachments (>15 KB) directly to the tablet's Gmail address — imported automatically every 15 minutes.
  - Tablet refreshes its photo list automatically every 5 minutes and shuffles the order.
- **Editing medications or times remotely:**
  - Open your bookmarked Parents' Tablet link on your phone/PC → tap **Edit** on Gene's or Peggy's column (or **⚙️ Settings**). Tablet syncs automatically within 5 minutes (or immediately on reload).
  - Every add/edit/delete sends an email copy and can be undone from **Edit → History**.
- **Fully Kiosk quick controls (on the tablet):**
  - **Reload page:** Swipe in from **left edge** → **Go to Start URL**.
  - **Exit to Android Settings:** Swipe in from **left edge** → **Exit App**.
  - **Return to Parents' Tablet anytime:** Tap Android's **`○` Home** button at the bottom of the screen.
- **In-person check (every 6–12 months):**
  - Wi-Fi OS auto-downloads are turned **OFF** so overnight reboots don't interrupt kiosk mode. When visiting, exit Fully Kiosk → **Settings → Software update → Download and install** if needed, then confirm `○` Home still opens Parents' Tablet.

### How to Use Your Family Tablet

Keep the tablet plugged into its stand — it stays on by itself all day! Use the **5 big tabs across the top** anytime:

1. **💊 Meds (Daily Pills)**
   - **Automatic switch:** When pills are due, the screen switches to your lists automatically and plays a gentle chime. *(Need 10 more minutes? Tap **Snooze**.)*
   - **Checking off pills:** Tap the **big square box `☐`** next to each pill you take — it turns **green `✓`**. Tap again if you checked one by mistake.
   - **Gene's 2-hour morning wait:** After checking your **Wake up** pill, wait **2 hours** before Morning pills — the yellow banner shows the exact time!
   - **Changing meds or times:** Tap **Edit** at the top of Gene's or Peggy's list *(Tom gets an email backup of every change)*.

2. **🖼️ Pictures (Family Slideshow)**
   - Plays family photos automatically all day.
   - **Bottom buttons:** Tap **`◀ Back`** to see a photo again, **`⏸ Pause`** to hold on a photo *(tap **`▶ Play`** to resume)*, or **`Next ▶`** to skip ahead.
   - **Left-side album buttons:** Tap **`All`** or any family folder name (like **`Boys`**) on the left side to switch albums.

3. **📞 Call (Phone & Video Calls)**
   - Tap **`📞 Phone`** under someone's name for a regular speakerphone voice call, or **`📹 Video`** for a Google Meet video call.
   - Use **`New`** to dial any phone number on the keypad.
   - **Getting back to the tablet screen after a call:** Tap the round **`○` Home** button at the very bottom center of the tablet!

4. **📝 To Do List (Shared Notes & Tasks)**
   - Tap **`Add`** (or **`🎤 Voice`** to speak a reminder out loud), pick **For** (`Gene`, `Peggy`, `Both`) and **Date/Time**, then tap **`Add`**.
   - Tap a task's square box **`☐`** to check it off when done.

*After a power outage or restart: tap the screen once (or tap **"🔊 Tap once to enable sound"**) so chime alarms can play.*
*Reminder aid only — always follow your pharmacy labels and doctor's instructions first.*
