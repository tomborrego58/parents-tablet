How to Use Your Family Tablet

For Gene & Peggy

Leave the tablet plugged into its stand — the screen stays on all day by itself. Tap any of the big tabs across the top to switch screens anytime:

💊 Meds Daily Medication Checklists
  • Automatic reminder: When pills are due, the screen switches to your lists automatically and plays a gentle chime. Need 10 more minutes? Tap Snooze 10 min.
  • Check off each pill: Tap the big square box next to each pill you take — it turns ✓ green. Tap again if you checked one by mistake.
  • Gene's 2-hour morning wait: After checking your Wake up pill, wait 2 hours before Morning pills — a yellow banner at the top shows the exact time!
  • Edit pills or alarm times: Tap Edit at the top of Gene's or Peggy's column (Tom gets an email copy of every change).
🖼️ Pictures Family Photo Slideshow
  • Pause or go back: Use the buttons at the bottom: ◀ Back to see a photo again, ⏸ Pause to stay on a photo (tap ▶ Play to resume), or Next ▶.
  • Switch photo albums: Tap All or any family button on the left side (such as Boys) to view a specific album.
📞 Call Phone & Video Calling
  • Tap 📞 Phone under a family member's photo for a speakerphone voice call, or 📹 Video for a Google Meet video call (then tap their photo in Meet).
  • Tap 📞 Phone under New to dial any phone number on a big keypad.
📝 To Do List Shared Reminders & Notes
  • Tap Add to type a note, or tap 🎤 Voice to speak a reminder out loud! Tap a task's square box to check it off when finished.
○
How to get back to this screen anytime: After finishing a phone or video call (or if another screen ever pops up), tap the round ○ Home button at the very bottom center of the tablet.
After a power outage: Tap the screen once so chime sounds are enabled. Reminder aid only — always follow pharmacy labels & doctor's instructions first.
